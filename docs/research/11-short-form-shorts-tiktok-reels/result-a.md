# 11 · Short-form: YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels

Source: Claude Opus research agents (3 researchers, 3 fact-checkers, 1 writer), 2026-10-12. Notes: notes/.

## How to read this file

- **Tags**: **DATA** means a measurement over a sample, an A/B test or a shown retention graph. **PRACTITIONER** means a stated practice with no data shown. **THEORY** means research or a model not tested on short-form video. A platform's own statement with no numbers is **PRACTITIONER (T1)**. A platform figure with a stated sample but no method shown is **DATA (T1, method not shown)**. One video's view count is never DATA.
- **Row IDs** in brackets point to rows in the notes: `M` = `notes/mechanics-first-second-retention.md` (items 1–3), `S` = `notes/structure-text-audio.md` (items 4–6), `X` = `notes/crosspost-funnel-myths-examples.md` (items 7–9 and the examples). Every row there has been fact-checked.
- **Timestamps.** CI-2023, CI-2024, CI-2025 and CI-VIEWS are Creator Insider videos (YouTube staff). Their timestamps come from caption tracks grouped into ~30 s blocks, so a quote sits somewhere in the 30 s after the cited time. CI-2024 = [Creator Insider, "A deep conversation about Shorts with Jenny Hoyos and Todd Sherman"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_tWy-_otnUc) (2024-12-09). CI-2023 = [Creator Insider, "YouTube Shorts Algorithm — Explained!"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3jsYK_-aRU) (2023-08-23, Old). CI-2025 = [Creator Insider with Jordan Howlett, Todd Sherman and Rene Ritchie](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LsJD6qPBP5o) (2025-07-31). CI-VIEWS = [Creator Insider, "Update to How Shorts Views are Counted"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-QM6O4yPfrI) (2025-03-26). VIDIQ-26 = [vidIQ, "We found the perfect VIRAL Shorts Formula!"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u5ZX-GhSots) (2026-03-13, per-line caption timing). Creator Science = [Jay Clouse's Jenny Hoyos interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=As7abwNhG7Y); its timestamps come from an AI-generated summary and were not checked against a transcript. Section D timestamps come from each platform's own caption track (yt-dlp, 2026-10-12) and mark when a spoken line starts.
- **Old** means before 2024 and possibly outdated. The canonical TikTok ranking text (2020) and Instagram's (2021/2023) are Old, but no newer primary text replaces them.
- **Ads, not organic.** Most of the official numbers on captions, audio and safe zones come from ads guidance and ad brand-lift studies. They are marked "ads".

| **Ranking signals** | **Viewed vs swiped away** is the Shorts counterpart of long-form impressions × CTR and "the actual metric in YouTube Analytics" (PRACTITIONER T1, [YouTube Blog, Rene's Top Five, 2024-04-04](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/renes-top-five-march-4-2024/)) [M1.1].
