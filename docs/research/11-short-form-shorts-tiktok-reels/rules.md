## Rules by segment

- 0-1 s: Frame 1 carries the premise in picture, readable muted (PRACT; A M2.10, C). Skip fades, logos, greetings (PRACT; C). Start with the meaningful word or impact, not a music lead-in (PRACT; C).
- 1-3 s: State the promise: shock, topic, expected ending (PRACT; A M2.6). Prove the premise; reduce confusion, keep outcome uncertain (PRACT; C).
- 3-15 s: Foreshadow, then act; deliver a first real reward (PRACT; A S4-1, C). Do not open on credentials or backstory (PRACT; C, A D20).
- Middle: Change information or state, not just cuts (PRACT; C, A M3.14 refutes timer). Chain mini-payoffs; do not cram (PRACT; A M3.11).
- Last 2 s: Pay the exact promise, then stop; trim the tail (PRACT; A S4-9, C). CTA mid-video at peak (A S4-2) or after payoff (C).

## Numbers

- 3 min max, Shorts (2024-10-15) and Reels (2025-01-21): T1.
- Swipe window: 1 s Hoyos; 1.5 s vidIQ drawing (no sample); 3 s Instagram model, TikTok ads; 6 s TikTok hook.
- Hoyos (channel-specific): target 90%+ retention; average ~34 s; last second cut 83% to 88% (n=1); 99.9% of views from feed.
- Lacombled (C only, T3, older): viewed-vs-swiped under 60% poor, 70-90% strong.
- Length: Adobe 46% of 507 creators say under 30 s (opinion); Galloway 5,400 Shorts, 33 channels, 50-60 s best ~1.7M (2023, Old); Colin and Samir 6/8 at 34-43 s (Q1 2022, tiny).
- Ads, not organic: Kantar ~4,000 ads +58% recall, +48% title, 2.1x awareness; 93% sound-on; 1.5 s faster non-music; Meta 47% of value in 3 s.
- Facebook 2016 feed: 1.7 s per item (Old, not video).
- Text: reading ~238 wpm (4 words/s) vs 5-10 words/s; 52-76 px captions (C convention).
- Funnel (Galloway 2023): 22.7 vs 16.9 subs per 10k views, reverses above 1M subs.
- Mosseri: about half Instagram video watched muted.
- Safe zones: see table, all unofficial except Meta ads.

## Refuted and myths

- Three-second law, 70% viewed-vs-swiped, 75-80% or APV 80%+: folklore (A, C).
- Pattern interrupt every 2 s: unsupported (A, C).
- Seamless loop bonus: unproven (A, C); APV over 100% is a loop artefact (A).
- 15-25% burned-in caption lift, 85% watched muted: unsourced (A).
- TikTok-logo Shorts demoted, watermark costs 90%: unverified (A); C: partly false.
- Delete and re-upload a flop: harmful (A).
- Mosseri "post 30-60 min before": misattributed (A).
- Creator Rewards means rank bonus; #FYP; exact hashtag counts: myths (C).
- Shorts kill long-form: audience mismatch, not penalty (A, C); "4x faster" fabricated (A).
- Shorts need no thumbnails: outdated July 2026 (A).
- Third-party safe zones disagree by 100-300 px (A, C).
