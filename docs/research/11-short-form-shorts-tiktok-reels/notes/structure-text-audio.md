### 4.2 Beats by length (synthesis of the claims above, not a sourced template)

| Length | Beats (sourced pieces in brackets) | Where the payoff sits |
|---|---|---|
| 15–30 s | Hook with the visual premise in frame 1 (4-1, 4-4) → one-line foreshadow (4-1) → 1–2 "but" turns (4-4) → payoff → hard cut (4-1). No recap needed. | Last 1–3 s. The end should flow into the start (4-1 "abrupt end" supports rewatch). |
| 30–60 s | As above plus a mid-video CTA at peak action (4-2), a visible progress indicator (4-2) and a **restated premise before the payoff** (4-1). | Last ~10% of the runtime, after the restated premise. |
| 60–180 s | No platform or analyst structure was found (see Gaps). Constraints that are sourced: the music bed is capped at ~90 s per song and Content ID claims block the Short (4-14); IG only recommended Reels ≤90 s before 2025-01 (4-15). Practitioner logic: chain several 4-1 loops (mini-payoffs), each restating the premise for late arrivals (4-2 recap rule). | Mini-payoffs throughout, with the main payoff at the end. Front-loaded value (4-18) argues against holding everything back. |
