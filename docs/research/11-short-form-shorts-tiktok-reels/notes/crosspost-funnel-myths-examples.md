**Synthesis for the agent (THEORY, from 8.1–8.N3):** Shorts help a long-form channel when the Short's topic and promise match the long-form video linked from it, the link is said and pointed at, and the long video pays off in its first seconds. They hurt (empty subscribers, Shorts-only audience) when the Shorts topic differs from the long-form topic. For a POV-walk channel, that means Shorts from the same walks, each linked to its full walk.

### Negative and contested (9)
- 9.2 vs 9.1: YouTube says posting time is not a factor for non-news content, while TikTok lists posting time as an input. These are different platforms, so they don't contradict each other, but don't reuse YouTube's statement as if it covered TikTok.

- Instagram hashtag cap: some reports say Instagram cut the number of hashtags per post (to five) around Dec 2025. I could **not** source this to Instagram or Mosseri. See Gaps.
