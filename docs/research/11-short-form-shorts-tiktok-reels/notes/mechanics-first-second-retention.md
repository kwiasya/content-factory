## Gaps

1. **Web-search budget ran out.** The session-wide limit of 200 searches was shared across all agents and was reached after about 14 of this researcher's queries. The rest of the research used direct WebFetch of known primary URLs and caption downloads with yt-dlp. YouTube then rate-limited caption downloads, which blocked the Colin & Samir interview with Mosseri (`HDzhA_UFrnA`) and the Shorts-update videos `ziWKU7RWp4Y` and `7Yu4nR4FHLw`. A follow-up run with more search budget should cover gaps 2–6.
2. **TikTok 2024–2026 primary statements are missing.** The Creator Rewards Program's over-1-minute rule, the 10- and 60-minute upload limits, how TikTok Studio defines "watched full video" and "retention rate", and any FYF ranking update after 2022 were not verified. TikTok Support, Creator Academy and the Safety Center render with JavaScript and returned only page titles. The 2020 Newsroom text is still the newest primary ranking statement found.
3. **Instagram's primary ranking pages could not be opened.** The Meta Transparency Center page for the Reels chaining system (the > 95% same-length-watch and < 3 s predictions) is rendered with JavaScript, so its content comes from a search summary. The Mosseri statement on watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach (early 2025) is sourced only through t3n. Mosseri's own video or Threads post was not reached. Whether a "skip rate" metric was added to Reels insights in 2025 is unverified.
4. **No sample DATA on short-form retention curves, swipe timing or length vs completion** came from tier 2–3 analysts with published N (Paddy Galloway, 1of10, OpusClip-type studies). Every benchmark found was either unsourced (75–80% viewed, 80% APV) or a single illustrative curve (vidIQ, 1.5 s).
5. **First sound and first on-screen text have no short-form data.** The only official items are TikTok's ad guidance (5–10 words per second, proposition in 3 s, hook in 6 s; June 2025) and Instagram's down-ranking of muted or mostly-text reels. No measurement of voice-first versus music-first openings was found.
6. **Loops in YouTube engaged views:** a secondary source (TrueFuture) says engaged views exclude loops, but the YouTube Help text found only says that public views count replays. Unresolved.
7. **Conflicts to carry forward:**
   - Follower count is an input on Instagram but not a direct factor on TikTok.
   - Duration: YouTube normalises for length, while TikTok weights finishing a longer video more.
   - Thumbnails: YouTube said in 2023 that they don't matter, then launched custom Shorts thumbnails in 2026.
   - The swipe window is given as 1, 1.5, 3 or 6 seconds depending on the source.
8. **Older sources still canonical:** TikTok 2020/2022, Instagram 2021/2023 and Facebook IQ 2016 are flagged as possibly outdated, but no newer primary text replaces them.
9. **Teixeira, Wedel & Pieters (2012, *JMR*)** on emotion-induced engagement and ad zapping would be the classic tier-4 study for "joy and surprise early reduce skipping", but its DOI could not be resolved in this session, so it is not cited as a claim.
