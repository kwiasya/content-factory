## Title patterns

- Caught on camera: 4.41x (vendor); niche-bound.
- Turning X into Y: needs a real before/after; 3.22x (vendor).
- Inside X: access; 2.57x (vendor); fits POV walks (untested suggestion).
- Nobody is talking about X: 2.5x (vendor); high risk if undelivered.
- Forbidden (banned, abandoned): ~2x (vendor); misleading-promise risk.
- Duration ("100 days"): ~1.5x (vendor); high risk, copied.
- "I was wrong", "Everything you know is wrong": below average or burned out (vendor); saturated.
- C only ("30 Years in 2hrs 26mins", "Give me 9 minutes", "ACCOUNTANT EXPLAINS"): single-video multiples 9×–100×, unverified.

## Thumbnail rules

- Attention, trust, curiosity together; trust means shown things happen ([Chucky](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HP-rqigfw2s&t=74)).
- Closed mouth beat open mouth 30 of 30 tests, small gap, MrBeast team only ([Chucky](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HP-rqigfw2s&t=1298)).
- Open space around a small subject; claimed 3M vs 17M is a guess ([C&S-2027](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WyiGFFTnIS4&t=2971)).
- Show the famous item in the opening scene ([Film Booth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nv60Vww-YZ4&t=409), [old]).
- AI enhancement acceptable only if every element exists in footage ([C&S-2027](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WyiGFFTnIS4&t=3286)).
- Long-form 3840×2160, 16:9; Shorts 9:16 ([Help 72431](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431)).

## Numbers

- Impressions CTR: half of channels 2–10% ([Help 7628154](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7628154)); A tags DATA, no method shown; only benchmark; none for Shorts.
- vidIQ-4M (~4M 2026 uploads, vendor, method undisclosed): lifts 4.41x, 3.22x, 2.57x, 2.5x, 2.05x, ~2x, 1.77x, ~1.5x; question 0.82x; how-to 1.07x.
- YTGrowth (28,947 videos, vendor, vs channel median): year +9.5%, starts with number +5.1%, any number +1.3%, question −0.4%, caps 0%, brackets 0%, colon −1.1%; length r = 0.0046.
- Emoji, vidIQ 128M (vendor, correlational): Shorts +49% avg views; long-form −11% avg, +7% median.
- Length: Galloway ≤50 chars (practitioner); OverseerOS breakout median 55 (vendor); Japanese study 40–49 chars (one language, one niche); Briggsby, ViewsKit, Subsub unopened.
- Upworthy ([Robertson 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-023-01538-4)): +2.3% CTR per negative word; news headlines, not video.
- Closed mouth 30/30: reported, per-test numbers not shown, n=1 team.
- Viewed-vs-swiped: 70% average, 80%+ good, 85%+ excellent; Hoyos, stated, n=1.
- Test & Compare: few days to 2 weeks; no sample size published.
- Variants: 3 finalists; ~50 concepts (MrBeast); 30–40 titles (Galloway).
- Single observations: Veritasium ~10% better without "Surprising", asteroid ~14M views (n=1); YouTube 20% view drop, Facebook 80% survey (stated, no sample); Galloway "20% better title doubles views" (asserted); TikTok/Kantar +48% recall, +58% captions (ads, 2021).
- C only, unverified, no study named: +38% CTR with face; text −19% views, <10 characters, <7% of image; +20% median views for conflict; 2-week test, hundreds of views per variant, ~2x gap; multiples 9×–100×.

## Refuted and myths

- Arrows/circles penalized: no tier-1 source says so; A refutes C's "algorithms demote".
- Algorithm counts faces: myth (Chucky); C's "+38% CTR if face" has no source and A finds face value viewer-dependent.
- Changing a thumbnail re-triggers the algorithm: myth ([Chucky](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HP-rqigfw2s&t=1924)).
- A flop holds back the next video: myth; cost is audience memory.
- Test picks the CTR winner: wrong, watch-time share ([Help 13861714](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714)); C is right here.
- Test one change at a time (C): A says minute differences do not register.
- Views show Shorts hook strength: myth since 2025-03-31.
- Refuted inside A: "glance test" is a thumbnail check; Koh & Cui and Hoyos best-percentage claims unconfirmed.
- Upworthy style: retracted by its co-founder (2015).

## Disagreements

- Face: 1of10 (vendor, no data) says dominant; YouTube staff say viewer-dependent; C says +38%. A's T1 side has better evidence, none is measured.
- Title length: Galloway ≤50 vs YTGrowth null vs OverseerOS 55; A better, no clear winner.
- Shock faces: C says saturated; A has one creator's tests only.
- Minimal vs loud thumbnails 2026: unmeasured.
- Outlier copying: Galloway twist vs 1of10 change-subject.
- Idea vs execution: unmeasured both ways.
