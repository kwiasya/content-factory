**How this was sourced.** The session-wide web-search budget (200 queries, shared by every agent) was already used up when this share started, so **no web searches ran for these notes**. Instead:
- WebFetch on known primary URLs: YouTube Help, YouTube Blog, Google India blog, Instagram, TikTok, 1of10, ThumbnailTest.

**Tags** follow the CONTEXT block:
- **DATA**: a measurement over a sample, an A/B test or a retention graph is shown.

- **THEORY**: research or a model that was not tested on video.

**Timestamps** come from YouTube auto-caption tracks. A timestamp is the start of the caption line, or of a 10–14-line block, so treat it as accurate to about ±15 s. Podcast-page timestamps come from that page's own transcript or chapter list.
