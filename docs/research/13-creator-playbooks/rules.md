## Common and contradictory

Common (A):
- Packaging decided early (C1); idea before package (C2); package is a promise (C3).
- Premise early, one question open (C4); story is change (C5); don't fake (C6).
- Longer videos, fewer uploads (C8-C9; DATA descriptive).

Contradictions (A):
- Pacing: MrBeast doc "quick scene changes" vs MrBeast 2024 "let scenes breathe".
- Face: MrBeast, Harris vs Fireship, Kurzgesagt voice only.
- Script: Kurzgesagt, MKBHD vs MrBeast, Casey, Binsky improvised.
- Payoff: Kurzgesagt at 4% vs MrBeast at 82-84%.
- Retention: MrBeast doc, Hoyos "90%+" vs MKBHD, Casey, Trahan, Hoyos herself.
- Testing: Veritasium polls titles vs MKBHD rejects A/B.

Self-report only: every "why it works" claim; no sampled retention graph from any comparison creator, 2024-26.
