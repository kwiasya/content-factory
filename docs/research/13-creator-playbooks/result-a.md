# 13 · Creator playbooks: MrBeast and other schools

Source: Claude Opus research agents (3 researchers, 3 fact-checkers, 1 writer), 2026-10-12. Notes: notes/.

## How to read this file

- **Notes:** [mrbeast.md](notes/mrbeast.md) (items 1, 4, 5 for MrBeast), [harris-veritasium-kurzgesagt-fireship-mkbhd.md](notes/harris-veritasium-kurzgesagt-fireship-mkbhd.md) (item 2 for five schools, plus items 4–5 for them), [hoyos-neistat-binsky-yestheory-trahan-plus.md](notes/hoyos-neistat-binsky-yestheory-trahan-plus.md) (item 2 for five schools plus Nas Daily and Kallaway, plus items 4–5 for them). Every row below traces to a row in one of them; the row ID from the notes is given in brackets (e.g. `1.11`, `JH4`, `R8`).
- **Tags:** **DATA** = a measurement over a sample, an A/B test or a shown retention graph. Most DATA rows here are the researchers' own *descriptive* counts of channel catalogs (lengths, title patterns): they show what a creator did, never what works. **PRACTITIONER** = a stated practice with no data shown. **PRACTITIONER (observed)** = a researcher read the named video at the given timestamps; it shows the rule is used, not that it works (the third notes file calls this OBS). **THEORY** = research or a model not tested on video.
- **Timestamp provenance:** (captions) = YouTube auto-captions pulled 2026-10-12, about ±3 s; (chapters) = the creator's chapter list; [doc p.N] = page of the MrBeast PDF; [pods] = podscripts.co transcript markers, about ±2 min; [TD] = Transcript Desk edited transcript with block-level speaker labels; [CS page] = Creator Science episode page, start of the speaker block; [sozai] = SozAI transcript, section-level; [t07] / [t09] / [t10] / [t12] = timestamps carried over from this repo's research 07, 09, 10, 12 notes and not re-checked in this run.
- **◦** marks a row the notes give without a fact-check marker and without a flag. No checker disputed it, but none re-read it. Rows a checker flagged as "not opened" are left out of the tables and listed in "Refuted during verification".
- **Row IDs** repeat across the three notes files (e.g. `K1` is Kurzgesagt in one file and Kallaway in another; `C1` is Casey or a copy): where it matters the creator is named next to the ID.
- **(old)** marks creator evidence from before 2024 (possibly outdated).
- **Search caveat:** all three researchers started after the shared WebSearch budget was spent. Every source was opened directly (known URLs, Wikipedia reference lists, transcripts, YouTube Data API, yt-dlp listings). Breadth is lower than the prompt asks; see Coverage.

The MrBeast document is cited as [doc p.N]: 36-page PDF "How To Succeed In MrBeast Production", [copy read](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6623b7720b009050313e701c/66ede69453b7bbadcd2f05a8_How-To-Succeed-At-MrBeast-Production%20%282%29.pdf), [full-text mirror](https://www.alexanderjarvis.com/memo-how-to-succeed-in-mrbeast-production/).
