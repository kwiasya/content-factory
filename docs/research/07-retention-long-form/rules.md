## Numbers

- 50%+ still watching at 30 s = "above typical intros" badge (60 s+, 100+ views); the only YouTube figure. [h12942217](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12942217). C says none exists.
- Typical band = 10 latest similar-length videos (was "past year"). [h1715160](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1715160?hl=en)
- Mid-roll needs 8 min+; end screens need 25 s+ and fill the last 5-20 s. [h6175006](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6175006?hl=en)
- vidIQ: ~54% of long-form views engaged after the 2026-08-24 change (100 channels, method unpublished). [vidiqviews](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=We8TVsjs2B8)
- n=1 MrBeast: ~65% at 1:00 (APV 53.0%, AVD 7:24); 74% at 0:30 on a 9:00 video; old sponsor read 70% to 55%; last-100 average 13:37. [mbpdf](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6623b7720b009050313e701c/66ede69453b7bbadcd2f05a8_How-To-Succeed-At-MrBeast-Production%20(2).pdf)
- n=1 each, small channels 2022-23: Only Trails 80% at 0:30 (niche); Effxayy ~45% gone in 30 s; APVs 14.9% to 64.1%. [rr3](https://georgeblackman.substack.com/p/this-creator-had-70-retention-after)
- Galloway client data, sample unstated: AVD 4:35 to 8:00 (+77%), length +51%, TV share 23% to over 50%. [cs26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WyiGFFTnIS4)
- C only, no link: vidIQ 2026 ~60% at 30 s; APV ~30%, over 40% (5-15 min), over 50%; the guides conflict. Galloway 2022: 30-50% lost in 30 s, no method.
- C only, n=1: 70% at 0:30, AVD 4:01, ~40% APV (10:02 video). Colin and Samir: AVD over 40 min on TV vs ~10 on phones (own podcasts).
- edX MOOC, 6.9M sessions, 2012, not YouTube: median engagement at most 6 min. [guo](https://pg.ucsd.edu/publications/edX-MOOC-video-production-and-engagement_LAS-2014.pdf)
- Lab TV: over ~10 scene cuts per 2 min cut recognition. [kubey](https://www.simpletoremember.com/vitals/TV_Danger_SCIAM2.htm)

## Taxonomy verdicts

- Cold open → rename "promise confirmation" (the device is not the cause). Agree.
- Branded intro → keep if frictionless. Partial: A drops the stinger, C bans only value-free delay.
- Teaser / "coming up" reel → keep only if it adds a new hook. A only.
- "But first" before a sponsor → merge into sponsor integration. A: no help; C: no data.
- Fixed re-hooks at 3 and 6 min → rename "re-hook at transitions". Disagree.
- Pattern interrupt → keep only if it carries information. Agree.
- vidIQ intro patterns (explicit deal, stakes, contrarian claim, surprising stat) → add to hook library. A only.

## Refuted and myths

- Retention Rabbit figures and niche "good retention" bands: unverified, unsourced (A).
- 70% at 30 s and 50% APV as thresholds: myth (C).
- A slow first hour kills a video: refuted by YouTube staff (A).
- Zeigarnik as the open-loop mechanism: refuted by a 2025 meta-analysis (A); overstated (C).
- Algorithm pushes long or slow videos: unsupported (A and C).
- Spike means viewers loved it: false (A and C).
- Fixed cut, visual-change or wpm quotas: folklore (A and C).
- Shorts destroyed long-form attention: contradicted by Galloway AVD (A).
- Minute 1/3/6 as special checkpoints: heuristic only (C).
- Abrupt ending removes the end cliff: no, 20-30 point drops remain (A).
