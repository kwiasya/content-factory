## Frameworks

- But/therefore: Parker and Stone; Hoyos (unnamed). All lengths. PRACTITIONER.
- Three acts: Parker and Stone; Harris rejects it (C). Setup, complication, resolution. 10+ min. THEORY (Boyd).
- Story circle: Harmon (TV); no YouTuber on record. Need, go, find, take, return, change. 10-30 min. THEORY.
- Stakes, escalation, payoff: MrBeast memo, Galloway tiers. Each segment costlier, biggest last. 8-25 min. PRACTITIONER.
- MrBeast minute plan: memo. Promise 0-1 min, re-engage ~3:00 and ~6:00, weaker back half, abrupt end. 10-15 min. PRACTITIONER.
- Promise, journey, answer: Harris (analyst's label; C: he rejects formulas). 15-40 min. PRACTITIONER.
- Misconception-first: Veritasium, Muller. Commit, wrong model, refute, correct model, answer. 10-20 min. DATA (learning only).
- Kurzgesagt: no published template. Observed: intuition, early answer, "only half", zoom, wonder. 8-15 min. PRACTITIONER.
- Tutorial: Proof, steps with checkpoints, verify. 3-10 min. DATA (MOOC).
- Hook, foreshadow, but/therefore, last line (Hoyos); setup/rehook cycle (Kallaway, Wall). Shorts. PRACTITIONER (A only).
- Investigation, topic blocks, build progression: Veritasium, Harris, MrBeast. 20-35 min. DATA n=1.

## Beat templates

Practitioner defaults, not benchmarks. L1 main loop, Lr re-engagement, Lb block loop; unmarked beats keep L1 open.

**60 s.** A: 0-1 s hook frame (L1); 1-3 foreshadow; rehook by 5; 4-7 beats of 5-10 s (L1 + micro-loop); last 3-5 payoff; no outro. C: 0-3 promise (1 loop); 3-8 goal; 8-20 first step (+1 local); 20-35 complication; 35-49 decisive test; 49-57 payoff; 57-60 consequence or CTA.

**3 min.** A (derived, untested): 0-6 s hook, promise; progress by 0:30; 3-5 steps of 25-40 s (L1 + Lr ~1:20); last 10-20 s payoff closes L1. C: 0-5 s cold open; 5-20 goal; 20-55 attempt (+1 local); 55-1:15 mini-payoff; 1:15-1:55 escalation; 1:55-2:30 hardest beat; 2:30-2:50 answer; 2:50-3:00 meaning.

**10 min.** A (memo): 0-0:30 promise delivered; 0:30-1:00 stakes; 1-3 progression; ~3:00 +Lr1; 3-6 best content; ~6:00 +Lr2; 6-9 explanations; last 30-60 s payoff, abrupt end. C: 0-0:15 promise, proof; 0:15-0:45 rules; 0:45-2:00 attempt; 2:00-3:15 first payoff; 3:15-5:00 escalation; 5:00-6:30 midpoint reversal; 6:30-8:15 hardest test; 8:15-9:30 main payoff; 9:30-10:00 end; C keeps primary + 1 nested, 0 at the end. A puts weaker content late; C the hardest test.

**20+ min.** A (% runtime, four videos): cold open 8-14% (L1); context 14-18%; early payoff ~30-35% (+Lb); 3-7 blocks of 2-8 min (one Lb each); main reveal 73-90% closes L1; epilogue 6-15%. C: 0-0:30 promise (1 loop); 0:30-1:30 rules (+1 nested); 1:30-4:00 chapter, own loop and payoff; 4-7 new constraint; 7-12 midpoint payoff; 12-18 deepest complication; 18-final 10-20% main payoff; last 5-10% denouement. Both: longer means more completed chapters, not more loops.

## Stakes and payoff rules

- Make stakes legible within ~10 s before escalating (money, time, risk, curiosity). PRACTITIONER
- Escalate by changing state, not volume. PRACTITIONER
- Cadence: memo ~3 min in 10 min; one 30-min video every 2-4 min (n=1); no universal interval. PRACTITIONER
- The main payoff must answer the packaging promise. PRACTITIONER
- Challenge payoffs sit at 78-97% of runtime; Kurzgesagt answers at 4% and chains loops. PRACTITIONER
- A mini-payoff must close something and open the next question. PRACTITIONER
- Peak and end dominate remembered value (liking, not retention). THEORY
- After the payoff end abruptly; sponsors follow it; long-form may bridge. PRACTITIONER
- Do not assert retention falls after a payoff. PRACTITIONER
- "Reward schedule" cadence is untested; ask what value each chapter delivers. THEORY

## No narration, no face

- Frame 1 works alone with sound off. PRACTITIONER
- Show the result first, then explain. PRACTITIONER
- Progress is a visible change of action or goal per beat. THEORY
- Carry a visible progress variable (distance, count, time, object state); text supplies causal links. PRACTITIONER
- Replay peaks sit on "it works" moments and new-space reveals, mid-video as often as at the end ([Rambalac](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C2lMOj2z_4U)). DATA n=1, confounded
- Text at most 20 characters per second. THEORY
- Evidence is long-form YouTube; Reels is analogy.

## Refuted and myths

- "Every minute better than the last": A, memo puts weaker content late; C over-literal.
- 3:00/6:00 universal: A, built on an 818 s average; median now 1,657 s.
- YouTubers use hero's journey or story circle: A and C none on record.
- Harris formula "question, journey, answer": C, he rejects formulas.
- Classic arc predicts success: A, Boyd 2020.
- Zeigarnik memory advantage: A, [2025 meta-analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeigarnik_effect).
- Variable-ratio dopamine and "re-hook every 20/30/60 s": C.
- Spike means great, dip is diagnosis, one benchmark, post-reveal crash, more cuts mean retention: C.
- Dropped by A's fact-check: Wirz cliffhanger study, Kang inverted-U.
