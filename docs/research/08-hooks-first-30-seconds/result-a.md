## How to read this file

- **Tags.** **DATA** means a measurement over a sample, an A/B test or a shown retention graph. **PRACTITIONER** means a stated practice with no data shown. **THEORY** means research or a model not tested on video. Platform Help pages, policies and staff statements show no data, so they are tagged PRACTITIONER and marked **T1**.
- **Text-headline DATA is not video DATA.** The only DATA on hook categories comes from text headlines (Kuiken 2017, Robertson 2023, Upworthy). It measures clicks on text, not retention after a spoken or visual hook. Moving it over to video is an assumption.
- **Timestamps.** Unmarked timestamps come from the video's YouTube auto-captions (about ±2 s). A "~" means the time was read from a SozAI transcript page and is approximate. Podcast times come from the podscripts transcript.
- **[old]** means before 2024 and possibly outdated.
- **Conflicts of interest.** Kallaway sells Sandcastles (which runs his "Hook Genius" report) and courses. vidIQ sells the Outliers tool its videos promote. 1of10, OutlierKit and Motion are tool vendors. None of their "we studied thousands of videos" claims shows the sample, so all of them are PRACTITIONER.
- **The MrBeast document** ("How to Succeed in MrBeast Production") is a 2024 leak. Passionfruit reports two ex-producers confirmed it; MrBeast's team never did. Links: [PDF](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6623b7720b009050313e701c/66ede69453b7bbadcd2f05a8_How-To-Succeed-At-MrBeast-Production%20(2).pdf), [text copy](https://www.alexanderjarvis.com/memo-how-to-succeed-in-mrbeast-production/).

Modifier: `authority`. Layers: `pattern_interrupt`, `audio_recognition`. Traps: `bare_number`, `fake_label`, `throat_clearing`, `borrowed_opening`.

**Do not score a hook by its category.** None of Kuiken et al.'s feature models predicted headline performance better than the baseline (DATA, [Kuiken 2017](https://doi.org/10.1080/21670811.2017.1279978)). Execution and alignment matter more than the label.

**The libraries behind the table.** Kallaway's 6 archetypes (Fortune Teller, Experimenter, Teacher, Magician, Investigator, Contrarian; [K-6arch 01:26–11:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xnOe8aA9Pmw&t=86s)); Kallaway's 6 power words ([K-pw](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S9FlxFv9dxg)); vidIQ's Stop-Stack ([V-stop](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5A6XVQUopYA)); vidIQ's Shorts styles ([V-shorts](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u5ZX-GhSots)); vidIQ's outlier rules ([V-pattern](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EyHJupH4Dr0)); Film Booth's intro tricks ([FB-sneaky](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nb9cQmHFLTs), [old]); Kane's Hook Point pillars ([Think Media](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GNHI13u8uBs), [old]). All are PRACTITIONER. "Hook Genius" turned out to be a report inside Kallaway's Sandcastles tool (Section C4.10), not a public library of hook shapes; hookgenius.app is now a Suno prompt tool.

## Section D · Hook critique checklist

Run the checks in order. A fail at steps 1–3 sends the hook back before the later checks matter.

| # | Check | Pass | Fail | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Idea and angle** | The idea survives a one-sentence pitch without the listener stopping to think. | The critique only reworks wording on a weak topic. | PRACTITIONER: [K-best 5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJ0OtNuUzls&t=326s), [CS-khare 26:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5TlPHtxsBIA&t=1569s) |
| 2 | **Contract with the package** | The first frame or first spoken line shows or says the subject of the thumbnail and title.
