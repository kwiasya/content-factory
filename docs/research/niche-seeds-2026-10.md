# Ниша: девушка в кадре, прогулки, мем-текст, мужская аудитория

Seed list for a Watchlist built around the author's Reels: a young woman in Berlin, on camera and filmed by her boyfriend, in 7–15 s vertical walk and trip clips. Each clip has an English meme overlay with self-irony and flirting, and the target is male viewers aged 18–60+. Collected 2026-10-12.

## Как собрано

- **Method.** I scraped YouTube search directly with the Shorts filter, ran about 90 queries, and resolved each result's owner through oEmbed. I then opened every candidate channel page to confirm the handle, the channel ID, the subscriber count and the latest Shorts. Every handle below resolved on 2026-10-12. Subscriber counts are YouTube's rounded public figures from that day.
- **What is NOT verified.**
  - **Male share of the audience.** YouTube does not publish audience gender. Every "male audience" claim below is inferred from topic (motorcycles, survival, "dating an X girl", flirty questions aimed at the viewer) and from how titles are framed. Nothing here is measured. Check comments, or ask creators, before trusting it.
  - **Instagram follower counts.** These are self-reported in the channel descriptions.
  - **Example Shorts.** Each one is a real video ID with the view count shown on that day.
- **Honest gap.** The exact intersection is rare on YouTube Shorts: a pretty walk, a woman on camera, a flirty or self-ironic meme overlay, and a mostly male audience. Many such accounts are Instagram-only. Queries built from the author's own phrases ("my type", "touch grass", "therapy vs a field") return mostly noise (see "Запросы"). The list is therefore built from five neighbouring clusters, each sharing two or three of the four traits. The **Fit** column says which.

Fit codes: **W** = woman on camera from the first second, **S** = scenic, walk or nature setting, **T** = meme or POV text with self-irony or flirting, **M** = framing or topic that pulls men (inferred).

## Запросы

2. **`dating a german girl`**: DW Euromaxx "POV: You try to pay on a first date with a German woman!" 621K, LizLaz "Dating in Germany" 1.2M, many "German girl" POVs. Directly relevant to a Berlin girl.
3. **`am i your type` / `would you fall in love with me`**: flirty direct-question Shorts (Elizabeth Williams, theanyamatusevich). The second phrase also matches a musical-theatre song, so expect noise.
4. **`would you like to kiss me` / `would you spend the day with me`**: the high-ceiling flirty-question hook (Darisha 37M).
5. **`me trying to find a boyfriend` / `why am i still single`**: self-ironic single-girl Shorts (Nadine Breaty 6.4M, Sophie Silva 5.7M).
6. **`boyfriend photographer pov` / `my boyfriend filmed me`**: the boyfriend-behind-camera format (NickandCarrie 5.3M, What Em Wore, Liv Swearingen).
7. **`outdoorsy girl` / `girl hiking pov`**: women alone in nature (Outdoorgirl Sofie, Madison Clysdale, findingfiona). Mixed with survival-hack channels.
8. **`girl from germany pov` / `berlin girl pov`**: Laura Ramoso, sara carstens, Nuki van Lent, DW Euromaxx Berlin POVs.

Tested and **not** useful (mostly noise: Minecraft, kids, Hindi memes, musicals): `my type touch grass`, `girlfriend pov walk`, `therapy vs nature meme girl`, `cancelled my therapist`, `girl in a field`, `mallorca girl`, `potsdam`. The author's own phrasing is not how viewers search. Use those phrases in overlays, not as discovery queries.

## Смежные форматы

Patterns that work with male viewers for a woman-on-camera creator. Evidence is view counts observed on 2026-10-12 plus secondary sources. "Male" is inferred unless a source says otherwise.

1. **A flirty direct question to the viewer** ("would you…?", "am I your type?", "so… you free this weekend?").
   - Darisha "Would you like to kiss me?" 37M on 2.22M subs.
   - Madison Isom "I heard u told your friends that I am not your type" 851K on a three-video channel.
   - theanyamatusevich gets 13–28K per Short on 1.24K subs.
   - Elizabeth Williams gets 51K on 735 subs.
   - This is the highest views-to-subscribers ratio in the whole scan, and it maps one-to-one onto the author's "so… you free this weekend? 😏".
   - The "POV: I'm your boyfriend" mirror format has been a named meme since mid-2019 ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/pov-boyfriend)). Commentators describe it as comment-driven because viewers reply with how they would react ([Virlo glossary](https://virlo.ai/glossary/pov-meaning-tiktok); [New Statesman](https://newstatesman.com/?p=84415)).
2. **The "dating a [nationality] girl" frame.**
   - Violet_Myr "Happy relationships in Eastern Europe" 379K.
   - Viki Overseas "Don't marry Russian girls" 487K.
   - DW Euromaxx "POV: first date with a German woman" 621K.
   - Jasmin and James "When your girlfriend is German 😅" 4.6M.
   - Content Machine "expectations dating a British girl vs reality" 2.8M.
   - The title talks to a man about dating a woman like the creator. A Berlin and Eastern European identity is an asset here: "dating a girl from Berlin be like…".
3. **Boyfriend behind the camera** (the author's real setup).
   - NickandCarrie "POV of Photographer Boyfriend" 5.3M.
   - Nastya Abroad "My BF TRICKED ME on vacation" 29M.
   - Liv Swearingen "Letting my Boyfriend edit the video" 6.6M.
   - The visual grammar goes back to the #FollowMeTo series, which Murad Osmann started by photographing his girlfriend leading him by the hand ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/digital/how-sweet-simple-instagram-photo-gave-rise-sweeping-global-travel-brand-172124/)).
   - Caveat: the couple accounts above probably skew female. For male growth, keep the boyfriend implied (hand, voice, "he said…") rather than on screen.
4. **Rating date spots / "take me on a date here".**
   - House of Highlights "Are These First Date Spots VALID?" 21M.
   - Sambucha "Should You Go Here On A First Date?" 1.7M.
   - Katie Feeney "RANKING FIRST DATES" 426K.
   - "Take me on a date, I deserve it" trend Shorts: Gianna Stardust 756K, lunamoon 8.8M.
   - A location-led dating series can carry a creator: Matthew Wurnig's "50 Dates 50 States" built 600K+ TikTok followers ([Dakota News Now](https://www.dakotanewsnow.com/2025/05/05/tiktok-star-kicks-off-viral-dating-series-south-dakota/)).
   - This fits the author's walks directly: "rating Berlin spots as a first date 🌳 7/10, would kiss here".
5. **Self-irony about being single.** Nadine Breaty 6.4M and Sophie Silva 5.7M. This works best when the joke is on her and the last line opens a door to the viewer, as in the author's "cancelled my therapist 💅" → "so… you free?".
6. **A woman alone in nature with a deadpan one-liner.**
   - Outdoorgirl Sofie "The forest is my favorite club 🪩" (55K on 58.9K subs).
   - Minsi Outdoors "My Neck Hurt So I Grabbed My Machete 😂" 278K.
   - pepper steps "women belong outdoors." 102K.
   - Outdoors topics pull men, so the overlay can stay light.

Platform note: secondary sources put YouTube Shorts viewers at about 56–58% male, versus a contested split on Reels ([mediacomponents](https://mediacomponents.com/instagram-reels-vs-tiktok-vs-youtube-shorts-a-side-by-side-comparison/); [mycvcreator](https://www.mycvcreator.com/blog/youtube-shorts-vs-instagram-reels-which-is-better-for-content-creators)). The two sources disagree, so treat this as rough. Cross-posting the author's Reels to Shorts is a cheap test for male reach.
