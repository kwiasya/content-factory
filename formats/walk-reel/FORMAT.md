# Format: Walk Reel

- id: `walk-reel`
- Platform: Instagram Reels first; YouTube Shorts and TikTok as reposts (caption, hashtags and safe zone change, the cut does not)
- Length: 15–60 s, vertical 9:16
- Source: material first: one Footage, Clips already selected by the Author; no voice yet
- Script: `cf-script/1` (`docs/script-schema.md`), written by `write-script` from the Shot log and `style.json`
- Face and voice: no face, no voice; text overlays carry the line
- Ending: seamless loop by default
- Style profile: `style.json`
- Hook library: `formats/walk-reel/hook-library.md` (generated by `hooks export`)
- Research rules that apply: 11 (short-form), 08 (hooks, the ≤3 s premise), 12 (first frame is the thumbnail)
- Pipeline: `/reel` (ingest-footage → analyze-clips → write-script → beat-grid → build-reel → render-reel)
